# CashConvert Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2026**

## Overview
CashConvert is a Chrome extension that shows live exchange rates, converts between currencies, and converts prices you highlight on web pages.

## Data collection
CashConvert does **not** collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What is stored on your device
- Cached exchange rates (refreshed every 60 minutes)
- Your chosen base currency and world converter currencies
- The text you right-click to convert, kept only for the current browser session

Nothing leaves your device except the requests below.

## Network requests
- **Frankfurter API** (api.frankfurter.dev, with api.frankfurter.app as a fallback) to fetch public European Central Bank exchange rates
- **Google Fonts** (fonts.googleapis.com) to load the Fredoka and Nunito typefaces

These requests contain no personal data and no page content.

## Page access
CashConvert cannot read web pages. It only receives the text you highlight and choose to convert from the right-click menu.

## Changes
Any changes to this policy will be published in this repository.
